CHAPTER 01 — 06:10

ORIGIN

Every cup begins somewhere else.

Before it is a drink, coffee is a place. Ours is a smallholding in the Gedeo highlands of Ethiopia — heirloom trees grown under false banana, cherries picked by hand in the week they turn red.

CHAPTER 02 — 06:25

ROAST

Heat changes everything.

Green coffee arrives hard and grassy. Twelve minutes in the drum and it is caramel, fruit and smoke. We roast small — twice a week — in a converted print shop two streets from the café.
